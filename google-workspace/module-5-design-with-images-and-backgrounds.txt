Images, Shapes, and Backgrounds

Module 5: Google Slides

Insert and replace pictures, add shapes, change a slide's background, and duplicate, reorder, and delete slides.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Insert an image from your computer, Drive, or the web, and replace it without losing its size and position
- Add a shape and text box to a slide
- Change the background of one slide, or of every slide through the theme
- Duplicate, move, and delete slides in the filmstrip


## Pictures make the point

 type: scenario
 role: You're finishing the Riverside Commons welcome presentation.
 text: The words are in place but the slides look plain. Denise sent you a photo of the Riverside Commons lobby wants the Who to Call slide to stand out and asks for a copy of the first-week slide that you can adapt for Elm Street Apartments.
 task: Add the photo a shape and a background color and organize the slides.


1. Select the slide, then select Insert › Image (or Insert image on the toolbar).
2. Choose where the picture comes from: Upload from computer, Stock & web (stock images, GIFs, stickers, and Google images), Drive & Photos, Camera, or By URL.
3. Pick the image and select Insert or Open. Drag the image to move it; drag a corner handle to resize it.
4. To swap a picture and keep its size and place, right-click it, choose Replace image, and pick the new source and image.

Note: Use only pictures you have the right to use: photos the company took, its logo, or images whose license allows it. A picture of a tenant or employee needs their permission before it goes on a lobby screen.


## Shapes and text boxes
- Insert › Shape (or Shape on the toolbar) offers shapes, arrows, callouts, and equations. Choose one, then click or drag on the slide.
- Insert › Text box adds text anywhere, outside the layout's placeholders.
- With a shape selected, the toolbar shows Fill color and Border color. Type while a shape is selected to put text in it.
- Arrange › Order brings an object forward or sends it behind others; Arrange › Align lines objects up.


## Change the background
1. Select the slide in the filmstrip.
2. Select Slide › Change background (or Background on the toolbar).
3. Next to Color, pick a color. Or next to Image, select Choose to use a picture.
4. Select Done to change only this slide, or Add to theme to use that background on every slide in the theme.

Keep enough contrast between the text and the background. Dark text on a light background (or light on dark) is readable on a lobby screen from across the room; mid-tone on mid-tone isn't.

## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: You want a new lobby photo in the exact spot and size of the old one. What's the fastest way? Answer: Right-click the old photo › Replace image. Replace image swaps the picture and keeps its size and position.

Check yourself: In Change background, you choose a light blue color. What does Done do? Answer: Applies it to the selected slide only. Done changes the selected slide. Add to theme changes every slide that uses the theme.
