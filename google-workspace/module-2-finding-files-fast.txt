Finding Files Fast

Module 2: Google Drive: Organize and Share

Search Drive by name and by the words inside files, narrow results with the Type, People, Modified, and Location filters, and use Recent, Starred, and Shared with me.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Search Drive for words in file names and inside files
- Narrow results with the Type, People, Modified, and Location filter chips, and clear them
- Use search words such as owner:, type:, and before: for exact searches
- Choose between search, Recent, Starred, and Shared with me to find a file in seconds


## Search first

 type: scenario
 role: You answer Denise's file requests all day.
 text: Denise calls from Riverside Commons: she needs the lease for apartment 210 the October rent tracker Priya keeps and the water shutoff notice she remembers editing last week. Clicking through folders would take minutes. Search takes seconds.
 task: Find each file with search and filters.


1. Click Search in Drive at the top of the screen.
2. Type a word from the file's name or from inside it, such as lease or shutoff, and press Enter.
3. Results are sorted by relevance. Click a column heading to sort another way.
4. To clear the search, click the X at the right end of the search box.

Drive searches inside Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides and many other files, not only file names. Searching for a tenant's apartment number can find a letter that mentions it.


## Filter chips
The filter chips under the search box narrow what you see. Use them with or without words in the search box. A chip turns blue when it is on. To remove one, open it and choose Clear; to remove them all, click Clear filters.

## Search words for exact searches

 type: table
 caption: Search words you can type in Search in Drive
 columns: 
  Type this
  To find
 rows: 
   \water shutoff\
   Files with that exact phrase (use quotes)
   notice -draft
   Files with notice but not draft
   owner:pshah@parkviewpm.example
   Files Priya owns
   type:spreadsheet
   Spreadsheets only
   after:2026-10-01
   Files changed after a date (YYYY-MM-DD)
   is:starred
   Your starred files


When you know where to look, the left side is faster than search: Recent for what you opened lately, Starred for what you marked, and Shared with me for what others sent you. To find what is using your storage, open Storage and sort by Storage used.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: Denise needs any spreadsheet Priya owns. What is the fastest way? Answer: Use the Type chip set to Spreadsheets and the People chip set to Priya Shah. Two chips together narrow Drive to exactly the spreadsheets Priya owns.

Check yourself: Which search finds files that contain the exact phrase water shutoff? Answer: "water shutoff". Quotes match the exact phrase.
