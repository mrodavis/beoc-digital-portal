Invite Guests, Find a Time, and Answer Invitations

Module 7: Google Calendar & Google Meet

Invite coworkers and clients, pick a time everyone is free with Find a time, add a Google Meet link, answer invitations, and schedule across time zones.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Add guests to an event, mark a guest optional, choose guest permissions, and send invitations
- Use Find a time to choose a time when your guests are free
- Add Google Meet video conferencing to an event
- Answer an invitation with Yes, No, or Maybe, and know where to add a note or propose a new time
- Schedule an event in another time zone and show a secondary time zone on your calendar


## Invite guests
 type: scenario
 role: You schedule meetings for Parkview's office.
 text: Denise wants a lease renewal review with Priya on Thursday, at the first hour all three of you are free. Grace Holloway, the owner of Riverside Commons, lives in Los Angeles and needs a call at a time that makes sense for her. And two invitations are waiting for your answer.
 task: Schedule meetings people can actually attend, and answer invitations so organizers know who's coming.

1. Open the event's full page (create the event and select More options, or open an event and select Edit event).
2. On the right, under Guests, start typing a name or email and choose the person.
3. To make someone optional, select Mark optional next to their name.
4. Under Guest permissions, choose whether guests can modify the event, invite others, or see the guest list.
5. Select Save. Calendar asks whether to send invitation emails to the guests; choose Send to email them.

Note: Guests answer Yes, No, or Maybe from the invitation email or from their own calendar. You'll see their answers on the event.


## Find a time
After you add guests on the event page, open the Find a time tab. It shows your calendar and your guests' calendars side by side for that day, with busy times blocked out. Click a time when everyone is free and the event moves there. You can only see someone's busy times if they share their calendar with you or their calendar is shared within your organization; if not, you can ask them to share it.

 type: worked-example
 title: Scheduling the lease renewal review
 task: Find the first free hour after 1:00 PM on Thursday for you, Denise, and Priya.
 steps:
   move: Create the event, name it, set Thursday, and select More options.
   why: Find a time is on the full event page.
   move: Add Denise and Priya under Guests.
   why: Find a time can only show people who are on the guest list.
   move: Open Find a time and look at the three columns after 1:00 PM.
   why: Denise is busy 1:00 to 2:00 and Priya is busy 3:00 to 4:00, so 2:00 to 3:00 is the first hour everyone is free.
   move: Click 2:00 PM, add Google Meet video conferencing, and select Save, then Send.
   why: The Meet link lets anyone join from wherever they are, and the invitation puts it on their calendars.
 result: A one-hour review on Thursday at 2:00 PM that everyone can attend, with a Meet link.
 takeaway: Add guests first, then use Find a time instead of trading emails about availability.

To add a video call to any event, select Add Google Meet video conferencing on the event. Calendar creates a meeting link that every guest can use; it appears on the event and in the invitation.


## Answer an invitation
Open the event on your calendar and choose Yes, No, or Maybe next to Going?. You can also answer from the invitation email in Gmail. To explain your answer, select the arrow next to Maybe and choose Add note; your note is visible to all invitees. If the time doesn't work, choose Propose a new time from the same menu, pick a different time, and select Send proposal. (Propose a new time isn't available for all-day events or events with more than 200 guests.)

Practice: Drag the best response onto each invitation.


## Time zones
Calendar shows events in your time zone, and each guest sees the event in their own time zone. When you schedule with someone in another zone, you can set the event in their zone: on the event page, select Time zone next to the time and choose theirs. To keep another zone in view, open Settings › General › Time zone, check Show additional time zones, and choose a Secondary time zone. It appears as a second column of times next to yours.

 type: table
 caption: One call, two clocks
 columns:
   Grace Holloway in Los Angeles sees
   You in New York see
 rows:
    1:00 PM to 1:30 PM Pacific Time
    4:00 PM to 4:30 PM Eastern Time

Note: Before you send an invitation across time zones, check the time on your own calendar. A call set for 1:00 PM in Los Angeles is 4:00 PM in New York.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: Find a time shows Priya's whole day as unknown instead of free or busy. Why? Answer: Priya hasn't shared her calendar with you. You can only see busy times on calendars shared with you or within your organization. You can ask Priya to share hers.

Check yourself: You can attend an owner call but might be 10 minutes late. What's the best answer? Answer: Yes, with a note that you may be 10 minutes late. Answer and add a note (arrow next to Maybe › Add note) so the organizer knows what to expect.

Check yourself: Where do you add a second column of times for Pacific Time? Answer: Settings › General › Time zone › Show additional time zones. A secondary time zone is a Calendar setting, and it shows on every day and week view.
