Bring It Together: Run a Team Project

Module 8: Working Together

Combine Gmail, Chat, Drive, Calendar, and Meet to start and run a small team project, choosing the right tool for each job.

- Decide whether an AI assistant such as Gemini is available and appropriate for a task

## Kick off a project
1. Reply to the request so the person knows you're on it. Reply, not Reply all, unless everyone needs your answer.
2. Create a space for the project and add the team (Chat › New chat › Create a space, then Add people).
3. Share the plan in the space as a Drive file, with the access people need (Commenter if they should only comment).
4. Schedule the kickoff in Google Calendar: invite the team, use Find a time if needed, add Google Meet video conferencing, and send the invitations.

Practice: Drag the steps into a sensible order.


## Keep it running
- Keep one conversation in one place. If a topic starts in email and moves to the space, say so in the email.
- Mention people only when you need them, and use @all rarely.
- Link to Drive files instead of attaching copies, so everyone has the latest version.
- After a meeting, write down decisions and who does what, and assign comments or tasks for the open items.
- When the project ends, name the final versions of its files.

## AI helpers: check before you use
Some Google accounts include Gemini, Google's AI assistant. In Gmail it can summarize a long conversation, suggest replies, and help write a draft. Which features you get depends on your account: on personal accounts without a paid plan, some Gmail features (such as summaries, Help me write, and suggested replies) are available only in the United States, while paid Google AI plans and eligible Google Workspace editions include more. Your employer may turn these features off.

Note: Nothing in this course requires Gemini. If your job allows it, follow your company's rules about what information you may use with it, and read and check everything it writes before you send it. You are responsible for the message, not the tool.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: You need to tell a vendor the repaint dates and keep a record. Which tool fits best? Answer: Gmail. Email is best for people outside the company and for anything that needs a record.

Check yourself: Kim and Marcus should be able to comment on the plan but not change it. How do you share it in the space? Answer: Share the Drive file with Commenter access. Commenter access lets them add comments without changing the plan.

Check yourself: Which Microsoft 365 tool matches Google Chat spaces? Answer: Microsoft Teams channels. Teams channels, like Chat spaces, hold a team's ongoing conversation and files.
