COURSE DESCRIPTION:
The course is designed as an introduction to the world of technology. We will examine advancements based on current driving forces for the development of new technology and its practical applications in societal contexts. Based on established knowledge in human-computer interaction, the course focuses on emerging technologies, which contribute to societal change and development. The course further focuses on the application of concepts intended to describe and understand the relationship between emerging technologies and their use at different levels in society.
LEARNING GOALS AND OUTCOMES:
Upon completion of this course, you will gain the following computer competencies:
Define and describe basic computer functions and computer hardware/software that support them
Perform computer system operations such as log on, shut down, start programs, and file management
Define computer networks and describe their purpose; understand principles of data communication
Describe the main features of ongoing digital technology development, emerging use and transformative processes in relation to societal development
Present and argue for or against the development of emerging technology both orally and in writing
Decide on appropriate methods for evaluating an emerging digital technology and justify the choice LEARNING MATERIALS, ONLINE RESOURCES AND SUPPLIES
