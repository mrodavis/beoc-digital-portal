COURSE DESCRIPTION:
In this 45-hour course on Database Management with Microsoft Access, students will acquire a robust skill set that empowers them to excel in various professional and academic endeavors. By mastering the intricacies of database design, implementation, and management using Access, participants will gain the proficiency needed to streamline data operations, enhance organizational efficiency, and make informed decisions based on comprehensive data analysis.
LEARNING GOALS AND OUTCOMES:
Upon completion of this course, you will gain the following computer competencies:
Database Design and Modeling:
Efficient Database Schema Design: Ability to design well-structured and normalized database schemas tailored to specific organizational needs, ensuring data integrity and efficiency.
Entity-Relationship (ER) Modeling: Proficiency in modeling database relationships using ER diagrams, enabling clear visualization and understanding of data structures.
Relational Database Concepts: Understanding of fundamental concepts such as tables, relationships, keys, and normalization, laying the foundation for effective database design and management.
Microsoft Access Proficiency:
Database Creation and Management: Competence in creating, modifying, and managing databases within Microsoft Access, including tasks such as table creation, data entry, and database maintenance.
Form and Report Design: Ability to design intuitive forms and comprehensive reports for data entry, analysis, and presentation, enhancing usability and accessibility of database information.
Query Construction and Optimization: Proficiency in constructing and optimizing queries to retrieve, filter, and manipulate data efficiently, enabling effective data analysis and reporting within Access.
