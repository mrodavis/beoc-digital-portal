Automatic Replies (Out of Office)

Module 6: Working Smarter and Safely

Write and schedule automatic replies for time away, with a separate message for people outside your organization, and turn them off when you're back.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Turn on automatic replies for a set time period
- Write a helpful reply for coworkers and a safer one for outside senders
- Limit outside replies to your contacts
- Turn automatic replies off


## A reply that does your job while you're away

 type: scenario
 role: You are leaving the Lakeside front desk for a two-day training class.
 text: Patients partner offices and coworkers will keep emailing you. Coworkers need to know who's covering; outside senders need a polite answer and a phone number without an announcement that you're away to every stranger who writes.
 task: Schedule automatic replies with one message for inside the organization and a shorter one for outside senders.


1. Select Settings (the gear at the top right) › Accounts › Automatic replies.
2. Turn on Automatic replies.
3. Check Send replies only during a time period and set the start and end dates and times, so the replies stop on their own.
4. Type the message for people in your organization.
5. To answer outside senders, check Send replies outside your organization. Choose Send replies only to contacts if only people you know should get it. Type the outside message.
6. Select Save.

Note: Classic Outlook: File › Automatic Replies. Choose Send automatic replies, Only send during this time range, and use the Inside My Organization and Outside My Organization tabs.


## What to say

 type: table
 caption: Two audiences two messages
 columns: 
  Inside the organization
  Outside the organization
 rows: 
   When you're back
   When they can expect a reply
   Who covers for you by name
   A general phone number or shared mailbox
   Anything urgent coworkers should know
   Nothing about where you are or why



 type: worked-example
 title: Writing the replies
 task: Cover Thursday and Friday at a training class.
 steps: 
   move: Inside: \I am at a training class and back on Monday October 26. For front desk help contact Omar Haddad.\
   why: Coworkers get a date and a name.
   move: Outside contacts only: \Thank you for your message. I will reply on Monday October 26. For scheduling please call (555) 010-2400.\
   why: Partners get a date and a number without details about your absence.
   move: Set the time period from Thursday 8:00 AM to Friday 5:00 PM.
   why: The replies stop automatically even if you forget.
 result: Everyone who writes knows what to do next and nothing private is shared.


Note: Keep outside replies short. Saying you're on vacation, traveling, or away from home tells strangers more than they need to know.

Practice: Sort each detail into the reply it belongs in.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: Where do you set up automatic replies in new Outlook? Answer: Settings › Accounts › Automatic replies. New Outlook keeps automatic replies in Settings › Accounts.

Check yourself: Why check Send replies only during a time period? Answer: The replies start and stop automatically. With a time period, you don't have to remember to turn replies off when you're back.
