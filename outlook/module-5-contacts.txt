Contacts in People

Module 5: People and To Do

Use People in new Outlook to add, find, update and remove contacts, add a sender straight from a message, and start an email from a contact.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Create a contact with the details a front desk needs
- Add a message's sender to your contacts from the profile card
- Find, edit and delete contacts
- Start an email from a contact


## Why keep contacts?
 type: scenario
 role: You keep the Lakeside front desk's outside contacts.
 text: Every day the front desk calls the imaging center, the lab, the supply company and three insurance plans. Phone numbers are on sticky notes, and when a scheduler changes, nobody updates them. A wrong number costs a patient a missed referral.
 task: Move those numbers into Outlook contacts that anyone at the desk can use, and keep them current.

Select People in the navigation bar on the left. All contacts lists your contacts; select one to see its details. Coworkers in your organization appear in Outlook's directory automatically, so contacts are most useful for outside offices, vendors and services.

Note: Contacts are for business contacts. Don't store patient information in Outlook contacts; patient demographics belong in the practice's EHR or practice management system.


## Add, find, edit, delete
- Add: People › Home tab › New contact. Type the name and details, then select Save.
- Add from a message: in Mail, select the sender's name to open the profile card, then More options (…) › Add to contacts. Add details such as a phone number and select Save.
- Find: type a name, phone number or company in the Search box.
- Edit: double-click the contact (or select it › Edit contact), make the change, and select Save.
- Delete: select the contact › Delete (or right-click › Delete), then select Delete again to confirm.
- Email a contact: select it and select Email. The To line is filled in for you.

Note: Classic Outlook: People (Ctrl+3) › Home tab › New Contact opens a Contact window; Save & Close saves it. To add a sender, right-click their name in the message › Add to Outlook Contacts.

Practice: Put the steps for adding a message's sender to your contacts in order.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: A new scheduler at the imaging center emailed you. What's the fastest way to add her as a contact? Answer: Select her name in the message › More options › Add to contacts. The profile card fills in her name and email address; you add the phone number and save.

Check yourself: Which of these belongs in an Outlook contact at a medical office? Answer: A vendor's direct phone line. Contacts are for business contacts like vendors and partner offices. Patient information belongs in the practice's systems.
