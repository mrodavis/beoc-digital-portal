Email Signatures

Module 2: Writing Professional Email

Create a professional signature, have Outlook add it to new messages and to replies, and insert a different signature by hand when a message needs it.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Create a signature in new Outlook from Settings › Accounts › Signatures
- Choose which signature Outlook adds to new messages and to replies and forwards
- Insert a signature by hand from the Message tab
- Decide what belongs in a professional medical office signature


## Your name on every message
 type: scenario
 role: You are setting up your mailbox at Lakeside Medical Associates.
 text: Denise wants every front desk message to end with the same signature: your name, your title, the practice name and the front desk number. For quick replies to coworkers, a full signature block on every two-line message is too much.
 task: Make a full signature for new messages and a short one for replies, and know how to swap them when a reply goes outside the office.

1. On the View tab, select View settings (or select Settings, the gear at the top right), then Accounts › Signatures.
2. If you have more than one account, choose the account the signature is for.
3. Select New signature and give it a name you'll recognize, such as Front Desk.
4. Type and format the signature in the editing box, then select Save.
5. Under Select default signatures, choose whether Outlook adds it automatically to new messages, to replies and forwards, or both. Leave them set to no signature if you'd rather add it yourself.
6. Select Save again.

Note: Classic Outlook: File › Options › Mail › Signatures. Select New, name the signature, type it in Edit signature, then choose it under New messages and Replies/forwards and select OK.


## Inserting a signature by hand
When you are writing a message, select Signature on the Message tab (in the Insert group) and pick one of your saved signatures. Use it when the automatic one isn't right, for example, to add your full contact details to a reply going to an outside office.

Note: Classic Outlook: in the message window, Message tab › Include group › Signature.


## What belongs in a work signature
 type: table
 caption: Professional signature checklist
 columns:
   Include
   Leave out
 rows:
    Full name and job title
    Personal phone number or personal email
    Practice name
    Quotes, emojis, or decorative images
    Main phone number and extension
    Your home address
    The confidentiality notice your practice requires
    Anything your practice hasn't approved

Many healthcare organizations add a short confidentiality notice to every signature, telling a reader who got the message by mistake to notify the sender and delete it. Use the exact wording your practice gives you; don't write your own.

Practice: Put the lines of a professional signature in the usual order, top to bottom.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: Where do you create a signature in new Outlook? Answer: Settings › Accounts › Signatures. In new Outlook, signatures are under Settings › Accounts › Signatures.

Check yourself: Which item does NOT belong in a work signature? Answer: Your personal cell phone number. Keep personal contact information out of work email. Use the practice's number.
