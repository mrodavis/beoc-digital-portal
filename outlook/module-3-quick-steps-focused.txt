Quick Steps and Focused Inbox

Module 3: Organizing Your Inbox

Bundle the actions you repeat into one-click quick steps, and train Focused Inbox so the mail that matters stays in front of you.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Create a quick step with more than one action
- Use a quick step on a message
- Turn Focused Inbox on or off
- Move senders between Focused and Other for good


## Quick steps: your repeated work, in one click

 type: scenario
 role: You process billing mail at the Lakeside front desk.
 text: Each time you finish with a billing message you do the same three clicks: mark it read open Move to choose Insurance & Billing. You do it thirty times a day.
 task: Turn those clicks into one quick step and set up Focused Inbox so billing and lab notices don't get lost under newsletters.


1. On the Home tab, select the arrow next to Quick steps and choose Manage quick steps.
2. Under Quick steps in Settings, select New quick step.
3. Name it, then under Choose an action pick the first action, such as Move to folder › Insurance & Billing.
4. Select Add another action for more, such as Mark as read.
5. Optionally choose a shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+5 through 9) and add a description that shows when you point to the quick step.
6. Select Save. Your quick step appears in the Quick steps menu on the Home tab.

Note: Microsoft notes that quick steps can't be undone with Ctrl+Z, because some actions, such as permanently deleting, can't be reversed. Test a new quick step on one message first.

Note: Classic Outlook: Quick Steps is a group on the Home tab with ready-made steps such as To Manager and Team Email. Select Create New in the group to make your own.


## Focused Inbox
- Turn it on or off: Settings › Mail › Message list › Sort messages into Focused and Other.
- Move one message: right-click › Move › Move to Other inbox (or Move to Focused inbox).
- Move a sender for good: right-click › Move › Always move to Other inbox (or Always move to Focused inbox). Future messages from that sender follow.
- Check Other once or twice a day. Important mail can land there until Outlook learns.

Practice: Decide where each sender's mail belongs for the front desk.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: Where do you create a quick step in new Outlook? Answer: Home tab › Quick steps › Manage quick steps. Manage quick steps opens the Quick steps page in Settings, where you select New quick step.

Check yourself: A newsletter keeps landing in Focused. What makes future issues go to Other? Answer: Always move to Other inbox. Always move to Other inbox applies to future messages from that sender.
