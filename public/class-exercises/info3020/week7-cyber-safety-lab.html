Exercise 5 · 15 minutes · 2 points

HIPAA at the Front Desk

Part A: PHI or not?

PHI is any information that can identify a patient and is about their health, care, or payment. Ask: could this point to one patient?

Part B: Find Jordan's mistakes

Read about Jordan: Jordan works at the front desk. Before lunch, Jordan presses Windows+L to lock the screen. A patient's intake form, with an insurance number on it, sits face up on the counter all morning. Jordan calls out in the waiting room, "Mr. Lee, are you here for your diabetes follow-up?" Jordan writes a patient's name and phone number on a sticky note and puts it on the monitor. Jordan looks up a neighbor's chart to see why she came in. When a caller asks about an appointment, Jordan first confirms the caller's date of birth. Later, Jordan emails lab results to "J. Smith" from autocomplete without checking the address.

Check every problem you find. Be careful. Two of these are things Jordan did right.
