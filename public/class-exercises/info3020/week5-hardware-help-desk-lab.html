Exercise 3 · 12 minutes · 2 points

Workstation Check

This one is hands-on. Sit the way you normally sit. Ask your partner to look at your setup. Mark each line. If the answer is No, try to fix it, then mark Fixed.

Exercise 4 · 15 minutes · 2 points

Write the Incident Report

What happened: It is 9:10 AM. The multifunction printer in the reception area will not send scans to email. The screen says Send Error. Dr. Patel needs a patient referral form scanned before 10:00 AM. You checked the network cable. You turned the machine off, waited 30 seconds, and turned it on. You also tried scan-to-folder. Nothing worked.

IT can only help quickly if your report is clear. Fill in every box. Use full sentences.

Exercise 5 · 10 minutes · 2 points

Video Call Ready?

Read about Jordan: Jordan joins the staff video meeting from the front desk 4 minutes late. The laptop sits low on the desk, so the camera looks up at Jordan's chin. A bright window is behind Jordan. Jordan tested the microphone before the meeting, but uses the laptop speakers with no headset, and people hear an echo. The patient sign-in sheet is on the wall behind Jordan. Jordan stays muted while other people talk. When asked to share a document, Jordan shares the whole screen, and the email inbox shows. Jordan eats lunch on camera.

Part A: Check every problem you find

Be careful. Two of these are things Jordan did right.
