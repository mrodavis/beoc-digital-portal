Your Job

The scenario: You are the office assistant at Lakeside Medical Associates. This week the office moved all of its files to Microsoft 365. Your supervisor gives you three new rules. Save your work in OneDrive. Share with a link, not an email attachment. Use Teams to talk with the team. In this lab you practice the new way to work.

Exercise 5 · 12 minutes · 2 points

Cloud Security Check

Read about Morgan: Morgan works at the front desk. Morgan turned on MFA, so signing in takes a password and a code from a phone. On Saturday, Morgan goes to a coffee shop and uses the free public Wi-Fi with no VPN. Morgan signs in to the work Microsoft 365 account on the coffee shop's shared computer and opens the patient schedule. A coworker texts and asks for Morgan's password to check one file. Morgan sends it. Next, Morgan shares a folder with an outside vendor, picks Can edit, and sets no expiration date. Morgan sees that a coworker who left the practice last month still has access, and removes that person. Then Morgan sends a file with patient information to the wrong person and decides to wait and see if anyone notices. Morgan closes the browser and leaves without signing out.
