Status, Presence, and Status Messages

Module 1: Getting Started with Teams

Learn what each Teams status means, when Teams changes your status for you, and how to set a status message that tells coworkers when you will answer and who to contact instead.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Explain what Available, Busy, Do not disturb, Be right back, Appear away, and Appear offline tell other people
- Describe when Teams changes your status automatically
- Set, reset, and time a status, and set a status message that clears itself
- Write a status message that is useful to coworkers and does not include patient information


## Why your status matters at the front desk
 type: scenario
 role: You are covering the check-in window at Lakeside Medical Associates.
 text: Twice this morning, Kevin messaged you a billing question while you were checking in a line of patients, then walked over to ask why you had not answered. Your Teams status said Available the whole time, so as far as Kevin could tell, you were ignoring him.
 task: Use your status and a status message so coworkers know when you can answer and who to ask in the meantime.

Your status (Teams also calls it presence) is the small colored dot on your picture. Everyone in your organization sees it next to your name in chats, channels, and meetings. It answers one question for them: can I expect a reply from this person right now? A status message adds a sentence of detail, such as when you will be back and who to contact instead.

## Automatic status changes
Teams also sets your status for you. During a meeting, Busy shows as In a meeting, and during a call as In a call, unless you chose Do not disturb. When you share your screen, Teams turns on Do not disturb so pop-up messages do not appear for everyone to see. If your computer locks or goes to sleep, Available changes to Away. When you pick a status yourself, it stays until you change it, until the duration you chose runs out, or until you select Reset status, which hands control back to Teams.

1. Select your picture at the top right of Teams.
2. Select your current status and pick a new one from the list.
3. To set it for a limited time, choose Duration, pick the status, choose when to reset it, and select Done.
4. To go back to automatic, select Reset status.

Practice: Drag the status that fits each situation.


## Status messages that help
A status message appears when people point to your name and, if you choose, when they start typing a message to you. Select your picture › Set status message, type the message, check Show when people message me, pick when it should clear under Clear status message after, and select Done. Choosing a clear time matters: a message that says “At lunch” is confusing at 4:00 PM.

 type: worked-example
 title: Writing a status message for a check-in shift
 task: Let coworkers know you are at the check-in window and who to call for something urgent.
 steps:
   move: Set your status to Busy.
   why: You can still glance at messages, but people should not expect an instant reply.
   move: Type: “Covering the check-in window until 1:00 PM. For urgent schedule changes, call ext. 210.”
   why: It says what you are doing, when it ends, and gives a backup for anything urgent. That is everything someone needs to decide whether to wait.
   move: Choose Today under Clear status message after, and select Done.
   why: The message disappears on its own, so you do not leave an out-of-date note for tomorrow.
 result: Kevin sees the note the moment he starts typing and calls ext. 210 for the urgent question instead of walking over.
 takeaway: A good status message answers three questions: what are you doing, until when, and who can help in the meantime.

Note: Everyone in the organization can see your status message. Never include patient details, such as “With Mrs. ___ about her test results.” Describe your task, not the patient.


## Knowledge check
Check yourself: You picked Do not disturb for training and forgot to change it. Two hours later coworkers think you are still unavailable. What is the best way to hand your status back to Teams? Answer: Select your picture › your status › Reset status. Reset status returns control to Teams, which sets your status automatically from your activity and calendar. Next time, choose Duration so it resets on its own.

Check yourself: Which status message is the most useful and appropriate? Answer: “At the check-in window until 1:00 PM. For urgent questions, call ext. 210.”. It tells people what you are doing, until when, and who to contact instead, without any patient information.
