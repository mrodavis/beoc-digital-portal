## Three homes for a file

 type: scenario
 role: You are the front office assistant at Lakeside Medical Associates.
 text: Three weeks into the job your OneDrive has eleven files with no folders a Microsoft Teams Chat Files folder you never created and two copies of the reminder call script. Maria asks for the latest version and you are not sure which one it is.
 task: Understand where Teams puts every file and organize your own files so you can always find the right one.


Teams does not have its own separate file storage. Every file you see in Teams lives in one of two Microsoft 365 places: your OneDrive (files that belong to you) or a team's SharePoint site (files that belong to the team). Where a file ends up depends on where you share it.

## OneDrive in Teams
Select OneDrive on the app bar to see your work files without leaving Teams. Home lists files you recently opened or edited. My files is your OneDrive, with your folders. Shared shows files others shared with you and files you shared. Favorites holds files you starred, and the Recycle bin holds files you deleted, so you can restore them. You can also browse files by People, Meetings, or Media type, and Quick access takes you to files in your teams' channels.

1. To create a folder: in My files, select Add new › Folder, type a name, and select Create.
2. To upload: open the folder, select Upload, choose the file, and select Open.
3. To move a file: point to it › More options (…) › Move to, choose the folder, and select Move here.
4. To rename a file: point to it › More options (…) › Rename, type the new name, and press Enter.

## Choose the right home
Practice: Sort each file to its best home.

Note: Lakeside's rule for files with patient information: keep them only in locations the practice approves and share them only with people who need them. Never move patient files into a personal OneDrive or a personal email account, and never upload them to a channel that includes people who do not need them.


## Knowledge check
Check yourself: You find a folder named Microsoft Teams Chat Files in your OneDrive. What is in it? Answer: Files you uploaded in Teams chats. When you upload a file in a chat, Teams stores it in your OneDrive in Microsoft Teams Chat Files and shares it with the people in that chat.

Check yourself: You deleted a file from your OneDrive by mistake. Where do you look first? Answer: The Recycle bin in OneDrive. Deleted OneDrive files go to the Recycle bin, where you can restore them.
