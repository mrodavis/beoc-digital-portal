Accessibility and Teams on Your Phone

Module 6: Working Smarter in Teams

Use the accessibility tools built into Teams (Immersive Reader, live captions, themes, zoom, keyboard shortcuts, and screen reader support), and use the Teams mobile app safely at work.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Open a message in Immersive Reader and use its reading tools
- Change the Teams theme and zoom level, and use keyboard shortcuts
- Turn on live captions and set noise suppression so others can hear you
- Use the Teams mobile app safely, keeping patient information private

Teams also has a Sign language mode for meetings with sign language interpreters. Your coworkers may use any of these tools. Writing clear posts with headings or numbered lists, and not relying on color alone, helps everyone who uses them.


## Use the tools

 type: worked-example
 title: Getting through Renee's privacy steps
 task: Read a long post comfortably and make sure you understood it.
 steps: 
   move: Point to Renee's post › More options (…) › Immersive Reader.
   why: It opens the text full screen with larger spaced-out text and can read it aloud.
   move: Use the text size controls and select Play to hear it read.
   why: Hearing and seeing the text together helps many readers not only people with dyslexia.
   move: Close Immersive Reader and reply to confirm what you changed at your desk.
   why: Replying shows Renee the steps were understood and done.
 result: You read the full post and confirmed it in the thread.
 takeaway: Use the reading tools whenever text is long or hard to read and write posts that work well with them.


Practice: Drag the tool that helps each coworker.


## Teams on your phone
The Teams mobile app for iPhone and Android uses the same work account, so your chats, channels, meetings, and files are the same as on your computer. It is handy for checking a schedule change or joining a meeting away from your desk. Your organization may require you to install it a certain way or may not allow work apps on personal phones; follow Lakeside's mobile device policy.

Note: A phone is easy to lose and easy for others to see. Lock your phone, do not open patient information in public places, and remember that notifications can show message previews on your lock screen.


## Knowledge check
Check yourself: Where do you open Immersive Reader for a Teams message? Answer: Point to the message › More options (…) › Immersive Reader. Immersive Reader opens from a message's More options menu.

Check yourself: Which shortcut shows all Teams keyboard shortcuts on Windows? Answer: Ctrl+. (period). Ctrl+. (period) shows the keyboard shortcuts list.
