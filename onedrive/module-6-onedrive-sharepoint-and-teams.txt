Where Files Live: OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams

Module 6: OneDrive with Teams, Outlook, and Office

Know where Teams stores the files you share in chats, channels, and meetings, find them in OneDrive, and add team folders to My files.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Explain where files shared in a Teams chat, a channel, and a meeting are stored
- Find Teams files in OneDrive: Microsoft Teams Chat Files, Recordings, Meetings, and Quick access
- Add a shortcut to a team library folder in My files
- Decide whether a file belongs in your OneDrive or in a team's library


## Where did that Teams file go?
 type: scenario
 role: You use Teams and OneDrive every day at Lakeside.
 text: Kevin sent you a billing spreadsheet in a Teams chat last week. Maria posted the updated procedures in the Front Desk channel. And the Monday huddle was recorded. Today you need all three, and you only have OneDrive open.
 task: Know where Teams put each file and find it from OneDrive.

 type: table
 caption: Where Teams stores files
 columns:
   You share a file…
   It is stored in…
   Who can open it
 rows:
    In a Teams chat (one-on-one or group)
    The sender's OneDrive, in the folder Microsoft Teams Chat Files
    The people in that chat (Teams sets the permissions)
    In a standard Teams channel
    The team's SharePoint site, in that channel's folder of the Documents library
    The members of the team
    A meeting recording (not a channel meeting)
    The organizer's OneDrive, in the Recordings folder
    Meeting participants, as the organizer's settings allow
    A channel meeting recording
    The team's SharePoint site, in a Recordings folder in the channel's files
    The members of the team

Note: In current Teams, a channel's files are on its Shared tab (it used to be called the Files tab). See the Microsoft Teams course for working with files inside Teams.


## Finding Teams files from OneDrive
- Files you sent in chats: My files › Microsoft Teams Chat Files.
- Files others sent you in chats: Shared › Shared with you (the Shared in column says which chat).
- Files from a meeting: Browse files by › Meetings, then the meeting.
- A team's library: Quick access in the left pane lists team libraries you use often. Open one to see the team's files.
- A team folder you use every day: select it and choose Add shortcut to My files.

Practice: Sort each file by where Teams stored it.

Note: When people leave an organization, their OneDrive is usually removed after a while, and the chat files and recordings in it go with it. Files the team must keep belong in the team's channel or library, not in one person's chat.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: You shared a schedule with two coworkers in a Teams group chat you started. Where is the file? Answer: In your OneDrive, in Microsoft Teams Chat Files. Chat files are stored in the sender's OneDrive, in Microsoft Teams Chat Files, and shared with the people in the chat.

Check yourself: The front desk team's procedures must stay available even if the person who wrote them leaves. Where should they be? Answer: In the team's channel or library. A team library belongs to the team, so the file stays when people come and go.
