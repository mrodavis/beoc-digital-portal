Files On-Demand: Online-Only and Always Available

Module 5: Syncing with Your PC

Read the status icons in File Explorer, keep important files on your PC for offline work, free up disk space, and use OneDrive when you are offline.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Identify online-only, locally available, and always available files by their icons
- Use Always keep on this device and Free up space
- Use Free up disk space and Download all files in OneDrive settings
- Prepare files for work without an internet connection


## All your files, without filling the drive

 type: scenario
 role: You are packing the laptop for the community health fair.
 text: The health fair is in a gym with no reliable Wi-Fi. You need the patient forms on the laptop but the laptop's drive is almost full mostly with old training videos you rarely watch.
 task: Make sure the forms are on the laptop for offline use and free up space from files that can stay in the cloud.


Files On-Demand lets File Explorer show every file in your OneDrive without downloading all of them. A file can be online-only (it takes no space and downloads when you open it), locally available (downloaded because you opened it), or always available (kept on the device on purpose). Starting with OneDrive build 23.066, Files On-Demand is turned on by default.

## Choosing what stays on the PC
- Always keep on this device: right-click a file or folder in your OneDrive folder and select it. Everything inside downloads and stays, so you can open it offline.
- Free up space: right-click a file or folder and select it. The files become online-only and stop taking space, but they stay in OneDrive and in File Explorer.
- For everything at once, open OneDrive settings (cloud icon › Help & Settings › Settings) › Sync and backup › Advanced settings. Free up disk space (the recommended choice) makes files online-only; Download all files keeps everything on the device.


 type: worked-example
 title: Packing for the health fair
 task: Keep the patient forms on the laptop and reclaim space from training videos.
 steps: 
   move: Right-click the Patient Forms folder › Always keep on this device.
   why: Every form downloads now and stays on the laptop so it opens in a gym with no Wi-Fi.
   move: Watch the Status column until each form shows the solid green circle.
   why: The icon confirms the files are really on the laptop before you leave the office.
   move: Right-click the Training folder › Free up space.
   why: The videos become online-only. They still appear in File Explorer and download if someone opens them later.
 result: The forms work offline and the drive has room again.
 takeaway: Always keep what you need offline; free up space on what can wait for the internet.


Note: OneDrive on the web also has an offline mode for work or school accounts on Windows and Mac computers with the sync app. It is on by default, and while you are offline you can still view, rename, move, and copy files and create folders in the OneDrive web app.

Note: Free up space isn't Delete. Deleting a file in your OneDrive folder deletes it from OneDrive everywhere (it goes to the OneDrive Recycle bin). Use Free up space when the goal is disk space.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: A file shows a blue cloud outline. What happens if you try to open it with no internet connection? Answer: It can't open until you are connected. Online-only files aren't stored on the PC, so they need the internet to download when you open them.

Check yourself: Your laptop is low on space. Which choice keeps every file in OneDrive but stops them from taking space on the laptop? Answer: Free up space. Free up space makes files online-only. They stay in OneDrive and still show in File Explorer.
