Signing In and Finding Your Way Around

Module 1: Getting Started with OneDrive

Sign in to OneDrive on the web and learn the layout: the left pane, the Create or upload button, the command bar, views, search, and the details pane.

By the end of this lesson you can:
- Sign in to OneDrive on the web and sign out on a shared computer
- Name what each part of the left pane shows: Home, My files, Shared, Favorites, Recycle bin, People, Meetings, and Media
- Use the command bar, the details pane, and the list and tiles views
- Search for a file by name


## Signing in

 type: scenario
 role: You are covering the check-in window at Lakeside Medical Associates.
 text: A patient is waiting while you look for the Office Phone List. You know it is in OneDrive somewhere but you are on a computer you have never used and every click feels like a guess.
 task: Learn the OneDrive layout well enough to find any file in a few seconds on any computer.


1. Open a browser and go to onedrive.com. Sign in with your work or school account (at Lakeside, your lakesidemedical.example address).
2. Or go to microsoft365.com, sign in, select the app launcher (the grid of dots at the top left), and select OneDrive.
3. Your OneDrive opens. A work or school OneDrive shows your name at the top of the left pane.

Note: On a shared or public computer, always sign out when you finish: select your picture or initials at the top right, then Sign out, and close the browser. Closing only the tab can leave you signed in for the next person.

Note: Microsoft is moving the Create or upload button from the top of the left pane to the right side of the page, next to the command bar. If you don't see it on the left, look at the top right of the file list. The choices in it are the same.


## The left pane, view by view
Home lists files you opened or edited recently, with filters for Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF. My files is your own OneDrive: your folders and files. Shared shows files others shared with you, and files you shared. Favorites holds files you marked with Add to favorites. Recycle bin holds files you deleted, so you can restore them. Under Browse files by, People groups files by the person you shared them with, Meetings shows files shared in your Teams meetings, and Media gathers your photos and videos. Quick access lists shared team libraries you use often.

Practice: Drag each description onto the view it belongs to.


## Selecting, the command bar, and the details pane
Click a file's row to select it. A round check box appears at the left of the row; select more check boxes (or hold Ctrl and click) to select several files. The command bar above the list changes to show what you can do with what is selected. Right-clicking a file, or selecting More actions (…) next to its name, shows the same commands in a menu. Select the file's name to open it.


 type: worked-example
 title: Finding the phone list in a hurry
 task: Open the Office Phone List and check who last changed it on a computer you have never used.
 steps: 
   move: Type phone in the Search box at the top and press Enter.
   why: Search looks through your OneDrive and the files shared with you so you don't have to remember which folder it is in.
   move: Select the row for Office Phone List not the name.
   why: Selecting a row picks the file without opening it so the command bar and details pane show its information.
   move: Select the information (i) button at the top right.
   why: The details pane shows who modified the file and when and who has access which tells you whether this copy is current.
   move: Select the file name to open it.
   why: Now you know it is the right file so opening it is the last step not a guess.
 result: The current phone list is open and you know who updated it last.
 takeaway: Search first select to check then open. It is faster than clicking through folders.


Switch to tiles view (the button at the top right of the list) when you are looking for photos or scanned documents. Switch back to list view to see dates, sizes, and sharing.


## Knowledge Check
Check yourself: Your manager shared a training document with you yesterday, but it isn't in My files. Where should you look first? Answer: Shared. Files other people share with you stay in their OneDrive, so they show up under Shared, not in your My files.

Check yourself: You finished checking the schedule on the computer in the patient education room. What should you do before you walk away? Answer: Select your picture or initials, select Sign out, then close the browser. Closing a tab can leave you signed in. Sign out, then close the browser, so the next person can't open your OneDrive.
